About me

I've seen what the numbers mean on the ground.

Some of my most useful data lessons didn't come from a classroom. They came from my family's business in Aurangabad, where I ran inventory, planned restocking, scheduled dispatch and kept suppliers on track. When the numbers were clear, decisions were easy. When they were messy, people guessed.

That pushed me deeper into analytics. Since then I've built a traceability database on a factory floor at Endurance Technologies, helped a five-person team score 45 Physical AI opportunities for Schneider Electric, mapped how an H-1B policy change showed up across 65,000+ Boston-area records, and trained my own small language model from scratch.

My approach is simple: start with the business question, not the tool. Then explain the answer so the person making the decision can actually use it.
