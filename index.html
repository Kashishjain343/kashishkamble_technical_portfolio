How I work

A clear question. Honest evidence. A decision.

Every project starts with a business question someone actually needs answered. Here's what that looks like in practice.

01 · Ask precisely

Did the H-1B policy change how people moved around Boston?

I tested it on 65,360 place-month records and was careful not to claim more cause-and-effect than the data could support.

 02 · Weigh the evidence

Where can Physical AI actually make money for Schneider Electric?

Our team scored 45 use cases on market size and Schneider's ability to win. I owned the predictive maintenance deep dive.

 03 · Make it usable

How can a factory find production data without digging?

I built a SQLite database and data-entry tool that replaced paper records for test-track results at Endurance Technologies.
