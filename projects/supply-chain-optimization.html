Supply chain optimization

An inventory allocation exercise for an FMCG market-entry scenario, connecting mathematical optimization with an operational decision.

Core task: Model how inventory can be allocated across warehouses while communicating the tradeoffs in a form a business team can use.

Business context

A growing FMCG operation needs to plan stock placement and replenishment across warehouses. Allocation choices affect availability and movement of goods.

Analytical approach

I used Python linear programming to represent the allocation decision and Power BI to visualize the resulting operational picture.

Skills demonstrated

Translating a business scenario into decision variables and constraints, comparing feasible allocations, and explaining results visually.

Why it matters

Good analytical recommendations account for operational constraints and remain clear enough for managers to act on.
