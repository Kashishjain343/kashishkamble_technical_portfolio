EV traceability & test-track tools

Internal tools built during my Endurance Technologies internship to make production and testing records easier to capture and inspect.

What I built

Java desktop interfaces backed by SQLite for traceability and test-track data entry, tracking, and record storage.

Operational impact

Automation improved access to production information and supported faster detection of delays. The internship resume reports a 50% improvement in operational visibility.
