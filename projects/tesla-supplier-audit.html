Tesla supplier audit analysis

A study of how supplier audit findings move from inspection to corrective action, considered alongside the Sustainable Supply Chain Initiative framework.

Project focus: The team mapped the supplier audit process to identify where information, handoffs, and corrective-action follow-up can affect visibility into working conditions.

Research approach

The project combined a semi-structured practitioner interview, secondary research on SSCI, and course materials. The interview informed a real-world process view; it was not an official Tesla or SSCI audit.

Mapped workflow

Supplier production and on-site audits lead to scoring and reporting, internal evaluation, and a supplier corrective action plan. The map highlights feedback loops when remediation needs re-evaluation.

Business question

Where might supplier audit and remediation processes lose time or visibility, and how can teams better connect formal standards with day-to-day implementation?

Deliverable

A value stream map and recommendations grounded in the interview and supporting research, with attention to process bottlenecks and data gaps.
